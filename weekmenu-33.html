Recipes

Monday

Chicken stir-fry with peppers and courgette

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

300 g free-range chicken breast chunks · 1 bell pepper (red/yellow) · 1 courgette · ½ onion · olive oil · pepper, sweet paprika (mild) · squeeze of lemon juice.

Method

  1. Cut the pepper and courgette into strips, finely chop the onion.
  2. Heat oil in a wok or frying pan, fry the chicken chunks for 5-6 min until cooked and golden.
  3. Add the onion, cook for 2 min.
  4. Add pepper and courgette, stir-fry on high heat for 5-6 min until tender-crisp.
  5. Season with pepper, paprika and lemon juice. Serve immediately.
The pepper comes from a 1+1 free deal — the second pepper is used on Wednesday.
Tuesday VEG

Veggie burgers with broccoli and yoghurt dressing

20 min · serves 2 · air fryer + hob

Ingredients

4 plant-based burgers · 1 broccoli · 150 g Greek yoghurt · lemon juice, pepper, salt · fresh chives (optional) · olive oil.

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Cut broccoli into florets, boil or steam for 6-8 min until tender-crisp (or air fry with a little oil for 8 min).
  3. Cook the burgers according to packaging (air fryer: approx. 10-12 min at 180°C, turning halfway).
  4. Mix yoghurt with lemon juice, pepper and chives into a dressing.
  5. Serve the burgers with broccoli and dressing.
Wednesday

Wholewheat pasta bolognese with beef mince

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

375 g beef mince · 1 bell pepper (leftover from Monday) · ½ onion (leftover from Monday) · 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g) · 250 g wholewheat pasta · Italian herbs, pepper, salt, olive oil · grated Parmesan (optional).

Method

  1. Cook the pasta according to packaging.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, fry the onion until translucent.
  3. Add the mince and fry until browned and crumbly, 6-8 min.
  4. Add the pepper, cook for 3 more min.
  5. Add half the can of chopped tomatoes and Italian herbs, simmer for 10 min. Season to taste.
  6. Serve over the pasta, with Parmesan if desired.
The mince pack is split across Wednesday and Friday, and the tomato can across Wednesday and Thursday — nothing goes to waste.
Thursday VEG

Poached eggs with chickpeas, lentils, spinach & feta

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

4 eggs · 280 g chickpea-lentil mix · 150 g spinach · 50 g feta · remaining chopped tomatoes (approx. 200 g) · remaining onion · pepper, sweet paprika, olive oil.

Method

  1. Heat oil in a frying pan, fry the remaining onion until translucent.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes and chickpea-lentil mix, simmer for 5 min.
  3. Add spinach and let it wilt, 2 min.
  4. Make 4 wells in the mixture, crack the eggs into them. Cover and poach for 6-8 min until whites are set and yolks still soft.
  5. Crumble feta on top, season with pepper and paprika.
The only legume/cruciferous dish this day, to spread gas-forming foods across the week.
Friday

Beef meatballs with aubergine and pepper

25 min · serves 2 · oven or air fryer

Ingredients

375 g beef mince (leftover from Wednesday) · 1 egg · 2 tbsp breadcrumbs (plain, unseasoned) · 2 aubergines · 1 bell pepper · pepper, salt, dried oregano, olive oil.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven or air fryer to 200°C.
  2. Mix the mince with egg, breadcrumbs, pepper, salt and oregano. Shape into balls.
  3. Cut aubergine and pepper into cubes, toss with a little oil.
  4. Place the meatballs and vegetables on a baking tray or in the air fryer basket.
  5. Bake for 15-18 min until the meatballs are cooked and vegetables soft, turning halfway.
