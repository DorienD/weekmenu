Mon

Tex-Mex Beef & Bean Bowls

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

300 g beef mince (from the 500 g pack — freeze the rest), 1 can kidney beans (400 g), drained, 1 red bell pepper, diced, 1 onion, chopped, 2 tomatoes, diced, 1 small iceberg lettuce, 2 tbsp plain Greek yoghurt, 1 lime, ground cumin, mild paprika powder, dried oregano, salt, pepper, olive oil.

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and sauté the onion until translucent.
  2. Add the beef mince and cook for 6-8 minutes, breaking it up as it browns.
  3. Season with cumin, mild paprika powder, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Add the bell pepper and cook for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Stir in the kidney beans and tomatoes and simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. Wash the lettuce and use the leaves as bowls.
  7. Divide the beef mixture over the lettuce leaves, top with yoghurt and a lime wedge.
Tip: freeze the remaining 200 g of beef mince for a quick meal later — so the whole pack still gets used.
Tue

Turkish-Style Baked Eggs with Spinach & Feta

VEG

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

4 eggs, 300 g fresh spinach, 100 g feta (or a mild white cheese), crumbled, 1 onion, chopped, 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g), mild paprika powder, ground cumin, salt, pepper, olive oil, fresh parsley.

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large sauté pan and soften the onion (no garlic).
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes with paprika powder and cumin, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the spinach and let it wilt.
  4. Make 4 wells in the mixture with a spoon and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover the pan and cook for 6-8 minutes until the egg whites are set.
  6. Crumble the feta over the top.
  7. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve straight from the pan.
Note: this dish is entirely garlic-free — don't substitute in a ready-made spice mix.
Wed

Asian-Inspired Chicken & Red Cabbage Stir-Fry

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

2 free-range chicken schnitzels, sliced into strips, 1/4 organic red cabbage, finely shredded, 1 carrot, in ribbons, 100 g chestnut mushrooms, sliced, 2 cm fresh ginger, minced, 3 tbsp soy sauce (garlic-free — check the label), 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp honey, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, sesame seeds.

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, sesame oil, honey and rice vinegar into a sauce.
  2. Heat a wok or large frying pan with a splash of oil.
  3. Fry the chicken strips for 4-5 minutes until cooked and lightly browned, then remove from the pan.
  4. Fry the ginger, carrot and mushrooms for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Add the red cabbage and cook for another 2 minutes until just tender.
  6. Return the chicken to the pan with the sauce, mix well and heat through for 1-2 minutes.
  7. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve immediately.
Tip: use the leftover cabbage and carrots later in the week as a fresh salad, so little of either pack goes to waste.
Thu

Italian Pasta with Lentil Ragù

VEG

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

250 g wholewheat pasta, 1 can lentils (400 g), drained, 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g), 1 carrot, finely chopped, 1 celery stalk, finely chopped, 1 onion, finely chopped, dried oregano, basil, bay leaf, salt, pepper, olive oil, 30 g grated Parmesan.

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion, carrot and celery (soffritto) for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes, oregano, basil and bay leaf.
  3. Simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the pasta according to the package until al dente.
  5. Stir the lentils into the sauce and heat through for 5 minutes.
  6. Season with salt and pepper and remove the bay leaf.
  7. Toss the pasta through the sauce and serve with the Parmesan.
This is the week's fixed carb day.
Fri

French Beef Traybake with Fried Egg

30 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

2 minute steaks (ca. 250 g total), 4 heads chicory, halved, 300 g baby carrots (leftover from Wednesday), 500 g small potatoes, halved (use about half the bag), 1 red onion, in wedges, dried thyme and rosemary, salt, pepper, 1 tsp mustard (garlic-free), 1 tsp honey, olive oil, 2 eggs.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Toss the potatoes, carrots, chicory and red onion with olive oil, thyme, rosemary, salt and pepper.
  3. Roast the vegetables for 20-25 minutes until tender and lightly caramelised.
  4. Meanwhile, mix the mustard and honey and brush it over the chicory halfway through roasting.
  5. Season the steaks with salt and pepper.
  6. Sear the steaks for 2-3 minutes per side in a hot pan with olive oil, for medium.
  7. Fry the eggs in the same pan.
  8. Serve the steak with the roasted vegetables and a fried egg on top.
