Mon

Middle Eastern chicken traybake

25 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

400 g organic chicken thigh fillet, diced; 1 aubergine, in chunks; 3 carrots, in half-moons; 1 red onion, in wedges; 1.5 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp sweet paprika, ½ tsp turmeric, salt, pepper; 150 g Greek yoghurt, ½ cucumber, splash of lemon juice.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 210 °C.
  2. Toss aubergine, carrot and onion with half the oil and half the spices on a tray; roast 10 minutes.
  3. Mix the chicken with the remaining oil and spices, add to the tray and roast 15 more minutes, stirring halfway.
  4. Dice the cucumber and mix with yoghurt, lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  5. Serve the traybake with the cucumber yoghurt.
Use pure spices rather than spice mixes: mixes often hide garlic.

Mild aloo chana palak with egg

30 min · serves 2 · hob · carb day

Ingredients

600 g potatoes, in 1.5 cm cubes; 1 can chickpeas (400 g), rinsed; 1 can chopped tomatoes; 250 g fresh spinach; 1 onion, chopped; 1 tbsp oil; 1 tsp each turmeric, cumin, coriander, sweet paprika; salt; 2 eggs; 100 g Greek yoghurt.

Method

  1. Boil the potato cubes about 10 minutes until nearly tender and drain; boil the eggs 8 minutes, peel and halve.
  2. Fry the onion in the oil for 3 minutes, add the spices and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes, chickpeas and a splash of water; simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Stir in the potato, then fold in the spinach by the handful until wilted (about 2 minutes).
  5. Serve topped with the egg and a spoon of yoghurt.
The whole can of chickpeas and tomatoes is used, so nothing is left over. This is the only legume day of the week.
Wed

Stuffed peppers with turkey, corn & cheese

30 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

2 large peppers (red or yellow); 400 g turkey mince; 1 onion, chopped; 1 small can corn (140 g), drained; 1 tbsp tomato paste; 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp sweet paprika; 1 tbsp oil; 100 g grated cheese. Salad: 3 tomatoes, ½ cucumber, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Halve the peppers, remove the seeds and pre-bake on a tray for 8 minutes.
  3. Fry the onion in the oil for 3 minutes, add the mince and cook until crumbly.
  4. Stir in tomato paste, spices and corn; cook 3 minutes.
  5. Fill the pepper halves, top with cheese and bake 12–15 minutes.
  6. Slice tomato and cucumber for the salad and serve alongside.
A mild Mexican-inspired flavour: no chilli, just cumin and paprika.
ThuVEG

Italian frittata with mushrooms, broccoli & feta

30 min · serves 2 · hob + oven

Ingredients

6 eggs; 250 g mushrooms, sliced; 500 g broccoli florets; 1 onion, chopped; 100 g feta; 1 tbsp olive oil; pepper. Salad: 3 tomatoes, 100 g feta, 1 tsp olive oil.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (grill or top heat).
  2. Boil the broccoli for 4 minutes and drain.
  3. Fry onion and mushrooms in an ovenproof pan with the oil for 6 minutes.
  4. Whisk the eggs with pepper, crumble in the feta and pour over the vegetables in the pan.
  5. Let it set on the hob for 3 minutes, then bake 8–10 minutes until golden.
  6. Slice the tomatoes, crumble over the last 100 g feta, drizzle with oil and serve with the frittata.
Broccoli is the only cabbage-family vegetable today, which spreads gassy foods across the week.
Fri

Air fryer meatballs with kale & carrot mash

30 min · serves 2 · air fryer + hob

Ingredients

400 g organic beef mince; 1 egg; ½ onion, finely chopped; 1 tsp sweet paprika, salt, pepper. Mash: 400 g potatoes, in cubes; 3 carrots, in pieces; 500 g chopped kale; ½ onion; 1 tbsp butter; 50 ml milk; 1 tbsp mustard (garlic-free).

Method

  1. Mix mince, egg, onion and spices and roll into 8 balls.
  2. Preheat the air fryer to 180 °C and cook the balls 12 minutes, shaking halfway.
  3. Boil potato and carrot in salted water for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the kale and cook 5–6 minutes more; drain.
  5. Mash coarsely with butter, milk, mustard and the remaining onion.
  6. Serve the mash with the meatballs.
Check the mustard label: some brands contain garlic.
