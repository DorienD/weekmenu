Recipes

Mon

Greek chicken traybake

25 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

300 g (organic) chicken breast, 1 courgette, 2 pointed peppers, 1 onion, 1 lemon, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp oregano, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Cut the chicken into 3 cm chunks and the courgette, peppers and onion into pieces.
  3. Toss everything on a baking tray with olive oil, oregano, salt, pepper and the juice of half a lemon.
  4. Roast for 20 minutes, stirring halfway.
  5. Serve with wedges of the other half lemon.
Use pure oregano, not a “Greek herb mix” — those can contain garlic.
TueVEG

Shakshuka with feta

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

4 eggs, 2 cans (400 g) peeled tomatoes, 2–3 pointed peppers (the rest of the pack), 1 onion, 150 g feta, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp sweet paprika, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion and peppers and fry for 5 minutes in olive oil in a lidded pan.
  2. Add cumin and paprika and fry for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes, break them up and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  4. Make 4 wells, crack an egg into each and put the lid on.
  5. Cook 5–6 minutes until the whites are set.
  6. Crumble the feta on top and serve straight away.
Sweet paprika is not hot; avoid smoked-hot paprika or chili powder.
Wed

Swedish meatballs, cream sauce

30 min · serves 2 · hob · carb day

Ingredients

300 g (organic) minced beef, 1 onion, 1 egg, 3 tbsp breadcrumbs, nutmeg, 800 g sweet potato, 375 g sprouts (half the bag), 150 g salad leaves (whole bag), 250 ml light cooking cream, 1 tsp cornstarch, olive oil, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Peel and cube the sweet potato and boil 15 minutes in salted water.
  2. Finely grate the onion and mix with beef, egg, breadcrumbs, nutmeg, salt and pepper; roll into 12 balls.
  3. Brown the meatballs in 1 tbsp olive oil, about 8 minutes.
  4. Braise the halved sprouts in a splash of water for 8–10 minutes.
  5. Drain the sweet potato and mash.
  6. Stir cream and cornstarch into the meatball pan and simmer 2 minutes to thicken.
  7. Serve with the salad.
Sprouts only appear on Wednesday and Friday, never on the same day as the chickpeas.
ThuVEG

Mild chickpea & mushroom curry

30 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

1 can chickpeas (240 g drained), 400 g mushrooms, 2 leeks, 1 courgette, 1 onion, 200 g sweet potato, 400 ml light coconut milk (whole can), 2 eggs, 150 g skyr, 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp mild curry powder (garlic-free!), salt.

Method

  1. Boil the cubed sweet potato for 10 minutes and drain.
  2. Boil the eggs for 7 minutes, cool and peel.
  3. Fry the onion, leek, mushrooms and courgette in olive oil for 6 minutes.
  4. Add the curry powder and fry for 1 minute.
  5. Add chickpeas, sweet potato and coconut milk and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  6. Serve with halved eggs and a spoon of skyr on each plate.
Check the curry powder for garlic — many blends contain it. No chili, so it stays mild.
Fri

Oven salmon, dill-skyr sauce

25 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

2 skinless salmon fillets (about 250 g total), 375 g sprouts (the rest), 1 lemon, 150 g skyr, 1 tsp dried dill, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Halve the sprouts, toss on a tray with olive oil, salt and pepper and roast for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the salmon, squeeze lemon over and roast another 10–12 minutes.
  4. Stir skyr, dill, a splash of lemon juice, salt and pepper into a sauce.
  5. Serve salmon and sprouts with the sauce.
Fish is an occasional change; the rest of the week is meat, egg and legumes.
