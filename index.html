Mon

Moroccan turkey tagine-style stew

30 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

400 g turkey breast, diced; 1 aubergine, diced; 4 carrots, sliced; 1 red onion, in half rings; 2 tomatoes, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground ginger, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp sweet paprika, salt; 100 ml water; juice of ½ lemon; 100 g Greek yoghurt.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pan and brown the turkey for 4 minutes; set aside.
  2. Fry onion, aubergine and carrot for 6 minutes, add the spices and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and water, cover and simmer 12 minutes.
  4. Return the turkey for 3 minutes, then add lemon juice and salt.
  5. Serve with a spoon of yoghurt.
Use pure spices, not ras el hanout: that mix often contains garlic.

Mild aloo chana palak with egg

30 min · serves 2 · hob · carb day

Ingredients

600 g potatoes, in 1.5 cm cubes; 1 can chickpeas (400 g), rinsed; 1 can chopped tomatoes; 250 g fresh spinach; 1 onion, chopped; 1 tbsp oil; 1 tsp each turmeric, cumin, coriander, sweet paprika; salt; 2 eggs; 100 g Greek yoghurt.

Method

  1. Boil the potato cubes about 10 minutes until nearly tender and drain; boil the eggs 8 minutes, peel and halve.
  2. Fry the onion in the oil for 3 minutes, add the spices and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes, chickpeas and a splash of water; simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Stir in the potato, then fold in the spinach by the handful until wilted (about 2 minutes).
  5. Serve topped with the egg and a spoon of yoghurt.
The whole can of chickpeas and tomatoes is used, so nothing is left over. This is the only legume day of the week.
Wed

French chicken in mustard-mushroom sauce

30 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

400 g organic chicken thigh fillet, in strips; 250 g mushrooms, quartered; 1 onion, chopped; 400 g green beans; 2 carrots, sliced; 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp butter; 2 tbsp mustard (garlic-free); 1 tsp dried thyme; 150 g Greek yoghurt; salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Cook the green beans and carrots 8 minutes until just tender; drain.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil for 6 minutes and set aside.
  3. Fry onion and mushrooms in the butter for 5 minutes.
  4. Return the chicken with thyme, mustard and 50 ml water; heat 3 minutes.
  5. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the yoghurt so it does not curdle.
  6. Serve with the vegetables.
Check the mustard label for garlic. Do not boil the sauce after adding the yoghurt.
ThuVEG

Italian frittata with mushrooms, broccoli & feta

30 min · serves 2 · hob + oven

Ingredients

6 eggs; 250 g mushrooms, sliced; 500 g broccoli florets; 1 onion, chopped; 100 g feta; 1 tbsp olive oil; pepper. Salad: 3 tomatoes, 100 g feta, 1 tsp olive oil.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (grill or top heat).
  2. Boil the broccoli for 4 minutes and drain.
  3. Fry onion and mushrooms in an ovenproof pan with the oil for 6 minutes.
  4. Whisk the eggs with pepper, crumble in the feta and pour over the vegetables in the pan.
  5. Let it set on the hob for 3 minutes, then bake 8–10 minutes until golden.
  6. Slice the tomatoes, crumble over the last 100 g feta, drizzle with oil and serve with the frittata.
Broccoli is the only cabbage-family vegetable today, which spreads gassy foods across the week.
Fri

Dutch beef hachee with red cabbage & apple

30 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

400 g organic beef mince; 3 onions, in half rings; 1 tbsp butter; 2 bay leaves; 1 tbsp mustard (garlic-free); 1 tbsp vinegar; 1 tbsp tomato paste; 200 ml water; pepper. Red cabbage: 500 g, finely sliced; 2 apples, diced; 1 tbsp vinegar; 50 ml water; pinch of cinnamon. Mash: 400 g potatoes, 30 ml milk, 1 tsp butter, nutmeg.

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes about 15 minutes in salted water.
  2. Simmer the red cabbage with apple, vinegar, water and cinnamon, covered, for 15–20 minutes.
  3. Cook the onions in the butter for 8 minutes until soft and brown.
  4. Add the mince and fry until crumbly, then add bay leaves, mustard, vinegar, tomato paste and water; simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Mash the potatoes with milk, butter and nutmeg.
  6. Serve the hachee with the cabbage and mash.
Red cabbage is the only cabbage-family vegetable today; broccoli is on Thursday.
