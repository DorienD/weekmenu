Recipes

Monday

Mediterranean chicken and vegetable traybake

25 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

300 g chicken breast, cut into chunks (freeze the rest of the pack for next week) · 400 g broccoli/carrot/cauliflower mix · 1 red onion, cut into wedges · olive oil, lemon, dried oregano, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Toss the chicken chunks with the broccoli/carrot/cauliflower mix and onion on a baking tray.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, sprinkle with oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Roast for 22-25 min until the chicken is cooked and the vegetables tender and lightly caramelised.
  5. Serve immediately, with an extra lemon wedge if desired.
An oven traybake instead of the usual stovetop stir-fry, for variety. The chicken pack is 600 g — freeze the remaining 300 g for another week.
Tuesday VEG

Warm lentil & feta salad with roasted pepper, cherry tomatoes & poached egg

20 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

1 can lentils (425 ml), drained · 1 bell pepper, chopped (save some for Wednesday) · 200 g cherry tomatoes · 40 g feta, crumbled · 2 eggs · red wine vinegar, olive oil, lemon, dried oregano · handful of walnuts (optional).

Method

  1. Preheat the oven (or air fryer) to 200°C.
  2. Roast the pepper and cherry tomatoes for 12-15 min with a little oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Warm the lentils briefly in a pan with a splash of water.
  4. Poach the eggs for 3-4 min in barely simmering water.
  5. Mix the lentils, roasted vegetables, feta, red wine vinegar, olive oil and oregano into a warm salad.
  6. Divide over 2 plates, top with the poached egg and finish with walnuts.
A warm protein salad instead of the usual egg bake or bean stew, for variety. The only legume dish this week — no cruciferous veg stacked on the same day.
Wednesday

Spanish-style rice skillet with beef mince

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

300 g beef mince (freeze the rest of the pack for next week) · 200 g rice · remaining bell pepper (leftover from Tuesday, approx. 100 g) · 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g) · 100 g frozen peas · smoked sweet paprika (mild), stock, olive oil, salt, pepper · lemon wedges to serve.

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large pan or skillet, fry the mince until browned and crumbly, 5-6 min.
  2. Add the pepper, cook for 3 more min.
  3. Add rice, chopped tomatoes, smoked paprika and stock, bring to the boil.
  4. Lower the heat, cover and simmer for 15-18 min until the rice is cooked, stirring occasionally.
  5. Stir in the peas for the last 3 min.
  6. Season with salt and pepper, serve with a lemon wedge.
A Spanish-inspired one-pan dish instead of the usual pasta bolognese, for variety. The mince pack is 750 g — freeze the remaining 450 g for another week.
