Recipes

Monday

Chicken traybake with Brussels sprouts, carrot and red onion (Middle Eastern)

25 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

300 g organic chicken breast, diced; 400 g Brussels sprouts, halved; 1 carrot, sliced; 1 red onion, wedges; 1 courgette, diced; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tsp paprika powder; ½ tsp cinnamon; salt; pepper. Sauce: 150 g Greek yoghurt, 1 tbsp lime juice, salt.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 220 °C.
  2. Toss the vegetables with half the oil and half the spices on a baking tray.
  3. Toss the chicken with the remaining oil and spices and spread among the vegetables.
  4. Roast for 20-22 minutes, stirring halfway.
  5. Stir the yoghurt with lime juice and salt and serve on the side.
Cumin and paprika are plain spices (no chili). Check that your spice jars contain no garlic.
TuesdayVEG

Mild shakshuka with aubergine and feta

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

1 aubergine, diced; 1 red onion, finely chopped; 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g); 6 eggs; 100 g feta; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tsp paprika powder; salt; pepper; fresh parsley or coriander, optional.

Method

  1. Fry the onion and aubergine in olive oil in a deep skillet for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add the spices and chopped tomatoes and simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  3. Make 6 wells and crack an egg into each.
  4. Cover and cook for 6-8 minutes until the whites set.
  5. Crumble the feta over the top and serve.
Protein: 3 eggs + feta per person. No legumes or cabbage today, gentle on the gut.
Wednesday

Beef meatballs with kale mash (classic Dutch)

30 min · serves 2 · hob + air fryer

Ingredients

300 g organic minced beef; 1 egg yolk; 1 tbsp mustard; ½ regular onion, finely chopped; salt; pepper. Mash: 1 kg potatoes, cut up; 450 g finely cut kale; ½ regular onion; 50 ml milk; 1 tbsp butter; nutmeg.

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes in salted water; add the kale for the last 5 minutes.
  2. Mix the beef, egg yolk, mustard, onion, salt and pepper and roll 10 small meatballs.
  3. Fry the meatballs until browned all over, 12 minutes in a pan or 10 minutes in the air fryer at 190 °C.
  4. Drain the potatoes and kale and mash with milk, butter and nutmeg.
  5. Serve the meatballs on the mash.
The week's carb day. Cabbage only today; legumes are on Thursday.
ThursdayVEG

Stuffed courgettes with chickpeas, feta and egg

30 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

2 courgettes; 1 can chickpeas (240 g drained); 100 g feta; 2 eggs; 2 tomatoes, diced; ½ red onion, finely chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp oregano; 1 tsp cumin; salt; pepper; 100 g Greek yoghurt on the side.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Halve the courgettes lengthwise and scoop them out; chop the flesh finely.
  3. Fry the onion and courgette flesh in olive oil for 4 minutes; add chickpeas, tomato and spices and cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Remove from the heat and stir in the feta and eggs.
  5. Fill the courgette boats, bake for 20 minutes and serve with yoghurt.
One whole can of chickpeas, no leftovers. This is the only legume day.
Friday

Asian chicken bowl with cauliflower rice and sprouts (garlic-free)

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

300 g organic chicken breast, strips; 1 cauliflower; 350 g Brussels sprouts, finely sliced; 2 carrots, julienned; 1 cucumber; 450 g washed spinach; 2 tbsp soy sauce (garlic-free); 1 tbsp sesame oil; 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated; juice of 1 lime; 1 tbsp olive oil.

Method

  1. Grate or pulse the cauliflower into rice.
  2. Fry the chicken for 6 minutes in olive oil with ginger and half the soy sauce; remove from the pan.
  3. Stir-fry the sprouts and carrot for 5 minutes; add the spinach until wilted.
  4. Fry the cauliflower rice for 3 minutes with sesame oil and the remaining soy sauce.
  5. Divide between two bowls, top with chicken and cucumber and squeeze lime juice over.
Check the soy sauce label for garlic. Sprouts are fine today since there is no other cabbage or legume on this day.
