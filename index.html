Recipes
Mon

Beef burgers with fried egg, green beans and mushrooms

± 20 min · serves 2

Ingredients

300 g AH (Greenfields) minced beef · 2 eggs · 250 g green beans (half the bag) · 125 g chestnut mushrooms (half the punnet) · 1/2 onion · olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Season the mince with salt and pepper and shape into 2 burgers.
  2. Boil or steam the green beans for 8-10 min.
  3. Meanwhile fry the mushrooms and onion in olive oil.
  4. Fry the burgers 4-5 min per side in a pan or air fryer.
  5. Add the fried egg in the last few minutes.
  6. Serve the burger with the fried egg on top, mushrooms/onion and green beans on the side.
Tip: save the other half of the green beans and mushrooms for Thursday, so both packs are fully used by the end of the week.
Tue VEG

Vegetarian Gouda schnitzel with soft-boiled egg, cauliflower, broccoli and tomato

± 20 min · serves 2 · oven/air fryer + hob

Ingredients

1 pack (2 pcs) vegetarian Gouda schnitzels · 2 eggs · 400 g cauliflower/broccoli florets · 500 g round tomatoes · olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Toss the cauliflower, broccoli and halved tomatoes with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast for 18-20 min at 200°C.
  3. Add the schnitzels for the final 10 min in the air fryer/oven (or cook separately per the packet).
  4. Soft-boil the eggs for 6-7 min, peel, and serve alongside for extra protein.
Note: check the label of the vegetarian schnitzels for hidden garlic in the breadcrumb/seasoning layer.
Wed

Free-range chicken chunks with chickpeas, aubergine and bell pepper

± 20 min · serves 2

Ingredients

400 g free-range diced chicken breast · 1 can chickpeas (400 g, drained) · 1 aubergine · 1 red bell pepper (half of the 2-pack) · 1/2 onion · olive oil, salt, pepper, mild paprika powder

Method

  1. Cut the aubergine and bell pepper into cubes and fry for 8-10 min in olive oil.
  2. Add the chickpeas and onion, cook 5 min more with the paprika powder.
  3. In a separate pan, fry the chicken chunks 8-10 min until browned and cooked through.
  4. Serve together.
This is the only legume dish of the week and contains no cabbage-family vegetables, to spread gas-forming foods across the week. A generous protein portion since the whole chicken pack is used (no waste).
Thu VEG

Lentil and tomato stew with bell pepper, mushrooms, green beans and a fried egg

± 20 min · serves 2

Ingredients

1 can lentils (400 g, drained) · 1 can Cirio chopped tomatoes (approx. 400 g) · 1 red bell pepper (rest of the 2-pack) · 125 g mushrooms (rest of the punnet) · 250 g green beans (rest of the bag) · 4 eggs · 1/2 onion · olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic-free Italian herbs

Method

  1. Fry the onion, bell pepper and mushrooms for 5 min in olive oil.
  2. Add the lentils and chopped tomatoes, season with herbs, salt and pepper, simmer for 8-10 min.
  3. Cook or steam the green beans separately (8 min) and stir through.
  4. Fry 2 eggs per person and serve on top of the stew.
Second vegetarian day of the week, not adjacent to Tuesday. Legumes are combined with extra egg here for enough protein.
Fri

Spaghetti bolognese with minced beef

± 25 min · serves 2

Ingredients

300 g AH (Greenfields) minced beef · 150 g wholewheat spaghetti · 1 can Cirio chopped tomatoes (approx. 400 g) · 1/2 onion · olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic-free Italian herbs · optional grated Parmesan

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the packet.
  2. Brown the mince with the onion.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and simmer for 10-15 min.
  4. Season with herbs, salt and pepper.
  5. Serve with the spaghetti.
