Monday

Middle-Eastern beef kofta traybake with feta, courgette and bell pepper

30 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

400 g organic beef mince, 1 egg, 1 onion (finely chopped), 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, ½ tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp mild paprika powder, 2 courgettes, 1 bell pepper, 100 g feta, 100 g yoghurt, 1 tbsp dried mint, salt, pepper, 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
  2. Mix mince, egg, half the onion and the spices into a firm mixture and shape 8 kofta rolls.
  3. Cut courgette and pepper into chunks and toss with oil, salt and pepper on a baking tray.
  4. Nestle the kofta among the vegetables and bake 18-20 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Stir yoghurt with mint and a pinch of salt. Crumble the feta over the tray and serve with the mint yoghurt.
Half of the yoghurt and feta is used here; the rest returns Tuesday/Wednesday. Spice blends may contain garlic: use pure single spices.
Tuesday VEG

Mild shakshuka with spinach and feta

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

6 eggs, 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g), 1 onion, 1 bell pepper, 300 g spinach, 100 g feta, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp mild paprika powder, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Soften the chopped onion and pepper strips in oil in a wide pan for 5 minutes.
  2. Add cumin and paprika and cook 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and simmer gently for 8 minutes.
  4. Stir through the spinach until wilted.
  5. Make 6 wells, crack an egg into each, cover and cook 6-8 minutes until set.
  6. Crumble the feta on top and serve.
Protein: 3 eggs + feta + spinach per person. This is the day without legumes or cabbage.
Wednesday

Chicken with dill yoghurt, roasted carrots and pointed-cabbage slaw (Scandinavian)

30 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

300 g organic chicken breast, 500 g carrots, ½ pointed cabbage, 1 apple, 1 cucumber, 2 hard-boiled eggs, 150 g yoghurt, 1 bunch fresh dill, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp mustard, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C and boil the eggs for 9 minutes.
  2. Cut carrots into sticks, toss with oil, salt and pepper and roast 25 minutes.
  3. Rub chicken with oil, salt and pepper and cook 18-20 minutes in the oven (core 75 °C) or 14 minutes in the air fryer.
  4. Finely slice cabbage, apple and cucumber and toss with lemon juice, mustard and some of the yoghurt.
  5. Stir the rest of the yoghurt with chopped dill and a pinch of salt.
  6. Slice the chicken and serve with carrots, slaw, halved eggs and dill yoghurt.
Cabbage appears only today and legumes only on Thursday, spreading out gas-forming foods.
Thursday

Pasta with roasted squash, white beans and turkey bacon

30 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

250 g pasta, 1 butternut squash (about 600 g, cubed), 1 can white beans (250 g drained), 1 pack lean turkey bacon strips, 1 onion, 40 g Parmesan, 1 tsp dried sage, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 210 °C and roast the squash cubes with oil, sage, salt and pepper for 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the pasta as directed and keep a cup of cooking water.
  3. Fry the turkey bacon and onion for 6 minutes.
  4. Mash half the squash with a splash of cooking water into a sauce.
  5. Toss pasta, sauce, remaining squash, beans and bacon together and heat 2 minutes.
  6. Grate Parmesan on top and serve.
This is the carb day. Use the whole can of beans: no leftovers.
Friday VEG

Mild coconut curry with tofu and cauliflower

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

500 g organic tofu, 1 small cauliflower, 1 bell pepper, 1 onion, 1 can coconut milk (400 ml), 2 tsp mild curry powder (garlic-free!), 1 tsp ground ginger, 1 tbsp tomato paste, 1 tbsp olive oil, juice of ½ lemon, salt

Method

  1. Cube the tofu and fry 8 minutes until golden; set aside.
  2. Soften the onion for 3 minutes, then add curry powder, ginger and tomato paste.
  3. Add cauliflower florets and pepper and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk plus 100 ml water and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Stir in the tofu and heat 2 minutes.
  6. Season with lemon juice and salt.
Many curry powders contain garlic: check the label. No chilli or sambal. Protein: about 25 g from tofu per person.
