Recipes

Monday

Middle Eastern chicken traybake with courgette and pepper

30 min · oven · serves 2

Ingredients

350 g organic chicken breast, diced, 1 courgette, 1 red pepper, 1 red onion, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp mild paprika, ½ tsp cinnamon, salt; sauce: 150 g quark, juice of ½ lemon, 1 tbsp dried mint or parsley

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 210 °C (410 °F).
  2. Cut the courgette, pepper and onion into chunks and toss with the chicken, oil and spices on a baking tray.
  3. Roast for 22–25 minutes, stirring halfway.
  4. Stir together quark, lemon juice, mint and a pinch of salt.
  5. Serve the traybake with the quark sauce.
Only use spice blends without garlic; single spices are safest.
Tuesday VEG

Mild shakshuka with feta

25 min · kookstel · serves 2

Ingredients

5 eggs, 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g), 1 red pepper, 1 onion, 150 g feta, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp mild paprika, fresh or dried parsley

Method

  1. Sauté onion and pepper in oil for 6 minutes in a deep frying pan.
  2. Add cumin, paprika and tomatoes and simmer gently for 8 minutes.
  3. Make 5 wells and crack an egg into each.
  4. Cover and cook 6–8 minutes on low heat until the whites set.
  5. Crumble feta over the top and garnish with parsley.
No chilli: flavour comes from cumin and mild paprika.
Wednesday

Swedish-style organic beef meatballs with sprouts and beetroot

30 min · airfryer + oven · serves 2

Ingredients

400 g organic minced beef, 1 egg, 1 onion (finely chopped), 1 tbsp oats, ½ tsp allspice or nutmeg, salt; 500 g Brussels sprouts, 250 g cooked beetroot; sauce: 200 g quark, ½ cucumber (grated), 1 tsp dried dill, lemon juice

Method

  1. Mix beef, egg, onion, oats and spices and roll into 12 meatballs.
  2. Air-fry the meatballs for 12–14 minutes at 190 °C (375 °F).
  3. Halve the sprouts and roast for 20 minutes with oil and salt in the oven at 200 °C (400 °F).
  4. Stir quark, cucumber, dill and lemon juice into a fresh sauce.
  5. Briefly warm the beetroot and serve everything together.
Cabbage-family today, legumes only on Thursday, to spread out gas-forming foods.
Thursday VEG

Mild lentil dahl with spinach and fried egg

25 min · kookstel · serves 2

Ingredients

1 can lentils (400 g), 250 g spinach, 1 onion, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground ginger, ½ tsp cinnamon, 200 ml coconut milk, 2 eggs, 150 g quark (topping)

Method

  1. Sauté the onion in oil with the spices for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the drained lentils and coconut milk and simmer for 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  4. Meanwhile fry 2 eggs.
  5. Serve the dahl with egg and a spoon of quark.
Protein from lentils, egg and quark: comfortably above 20 g on this veg day.
Friday

Italian pasta with turkey, mushrooms and spinach

25 min · kookstel · serves 2

Ingredients

150 g wholewheat penne, 300 g turkey breast strips, 250 g mushrooms, 200 g spinach, 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g), 1 courgette, 250 g Brussels sprouts (leftover), 1 onion, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp dried oregano or basil

Method

  1. Cook the penne in salted water according to the packet.
  2. Fry the turkey for 5 minutes in oil and set aside.
  3. Fry onion, mushrooms, courgette and sliced sprouts for 8 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes and oregano, simmer 6 minutes and stir in the spinach.
  5. Fold in the turkey and pasta and mix well.
Do not use ready-made pasta sauce: it often contains garlic.
