Recipes

Monday

Chicken stir-fry with peppers and courgette

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

300 g chicken breast, sliced into strips (freeze the rest of the pack for next week) · 200 g mixed peppers (save some for Wednesday) · 1 courgette · olive oil · pepper, sweet paprika (mild) · squeeze of lemon juice.

Method

  1. Cut the peppers and courgette into strips.
  2. Heat oil in a wok or frying pan, fry the chicken strips for 6-7 min until cooked and golden.
  3. Add the peppers and courgette, stir-fry on high heat for 5-6 min until tender-crisp.
  4. Season with pepper, paprika and lemon juice.
  5. Serve immediately.
The chicken pack is 600 g — freezing the remaining 300 g avoids waste for another week.
Tuesday VEG

Shakshuka with white beans, tomato and spinach

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

4 eggs · 1 can white beans in tomato sauce (370 ml), drained · 200 g vine tomatoes, cut into wedges (save some for Friday) · 150 g spinach · 40 g feta · olive oil, pepper, sweet paprika.

Method

  1. Heat oil in a frying pan.
  2. Add the tomatoes and white beans, simmer for 5 min.
  3. Add spinach and let it wilt, 2 min.
  4. Make 4 wells in the mixture, crack the eggs into them. Cover and poach for 6-8 min until whites are set and yolks still soft.
  5. Crumble feta on top, season with pepper and paprika.
The only legume dish this day — Wednesday has no legumes or cruciferous veg stacked on top of it.
Wednesday

Wholewheat pasta bolognese with beef mince & roasted cauliflower

25 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

300 g beef mince (freeze the rest of the pack for next week) · 100 g mixed peppers (leftover from Monday) · 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g) · 250 g wholewheat pasta · 1 cauliflower, cut into florets · Italian herbs (check garlic-free), salt, pepper, olive oil · grated Parmesan (optional).

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Toss the cauliflower florets with a little oil, salt and pepper; roast for 18-20 min.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the pasta according to packaging.
  4. Heat oil in a pan, fry the mince until browned and crumbly, 6-8 min.
  5. Add the peppers, cook for 3 more min.
  6. Add the chopped tomatoes and Italian herbs, simmer for 8-10 min. Season to taste.
  7. Serve the sauce over the pasta with the cauliflower on the side, with Parmesan if desired.
The mince pack is 750 g — freezing the remaining 450 g avoids waste for another week.
Friday VEG

Vegan burgers with lentil-tomato salad & yoghurt dressing

20 min · serves 2 · air fryer

Ingredients

2 vegan (oriental-style) burgers · 1 can lentils (425 ml), drained · remaining vine tomatoes (approx. 100 g), diced · handful of lettuce or lamb's lettuce · 100 g Greek-style quark · lemon juice, pepper, salt, olive oil.

Method

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Cook the burgers for 10-12 min, turning halfway.
  3. Mix the lentils, tomato and lettuce with a little oil and pepper into a salad.
  4. Mix the quark with lemon juice, pepper and salt into a dressing.
  5. Serve the burgers with the lentil salad and dressing.
