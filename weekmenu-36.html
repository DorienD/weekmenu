Recipes

Monday

Korean-inspired beef lettuce wraps with pickled carrot & cucumber

20 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

2 pieces round beef steak, sliced into thin strips · 1 head iceberg lettuce (leaves as wraps) · 150 g shredded carrot · ½ cucumber, cut into strips · fresh ginger, sesame oil, soy sauce (check garlic-free), rice vinegar, sesame seeds, spring onion.

Method

  1. Mix the carrot and cucumber with a splash of rice vinegar, let marinate briefly.
  2. Heat sesame oil in a wok or frying pan, fry the beef strips for 3-4 min over high heat.
  3. Add grated ginger and soy sauce, let it reduce until glossy.
  4. Wash the lettuce leaves and pat dry.
  5. Fill each lettuce leaf with the marinated vegetables and beef.
  6. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onion, serve immediately.
A wrap format instead of the usual stir-fry bowl, for variety.
Tuesday VEG

Oven-baked cauliflower & pepper frittata with feta

20 min · serves 2 · oven + hob

Ingredients

6 eggs · ½ cauliflower, cut into small florets · 1 pointed pepper, sliced · 50 g feta, crumbled · splash of milk, olive oil, salt, pepper, chives or parsley.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
  2. Parboil the cauliflower florets for 5 min, drain.
  3. Heat oil in an oven-proof pan, fry the pepper for 3-4 min, add the cauliflower.
  4. Whisk the eggs with a splash of milk, salt and pepper, pour over the vegetables in the pan.
  5. Crumble the feta on top and bake for 12-15 min until set and lightly golden.
  6. Let cool slightly, cut into wedges and serve with fresh herbs.
A frittata format instead of the usual omelette or shakshuka, for variety. The only cruciferous dish this week, with no legumes alongside it.
