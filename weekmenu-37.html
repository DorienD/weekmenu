Recipes

Monday

Spanish baked frittata with chicken sausage & pepper

20–25 min · serves 2 · hob + oven

Ingredients

6 eggs; 2 chicken sausages, sliced; 1 red bell pepper, in strips; 1 small (organic) courgette, in half-moons; 1 red onion, chopped; splash of milk; 50 g grated cheese; olive oil, salt, pepper, sweet paprika powder.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe frying pan and sauté the onion, pepper and courgette for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the sliced chicken sausage and cook for 3 more minutes.
  4. Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt, pepper and paprika powder.
  5. Pour the egg mixture over the vegetables and let it set for 2 minutes on the hob.
  6. Sprinkle with the grated cheese and bake for 12–15 minutes until set and golden.
  7. Let cool slightly, cut into wedges and serve with a fresh salad.
Note: pre-seasoned chicken sausage can hide garlic powder in the spice mix — check the label.
Tuesday VEG

Mediterranean shakshuka with chickpeas & feta

20 min · serves 2 · hob, pan with lid

Ingredients

1 tbsp olive oil; 1 onion, chopped; 1 red bell pepper, diced; 1 can chickpeas (400 g), drained; 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g); 1 tsp ground cumin; 1 tsp sweet paprika powder; salt, pepper; 4 eggs; 50 g feta, crumbled; fresh parsley.

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan and sauté the onion and pepper for 4–5 minutes.
  2. Add the chickpeas, chopped tomatoes, cumin and paprika powder and bring to a boil.
  3. Simmer gently for 8–10 minutes until the sauce thickens; season with salt and pepper.
  4. Make 4 wells in the sauce with a spoon and crack an egg into each one.
  5. Cover and cook for 6–8 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
  6. Crumble the feta over the top and garnish with parsley.
  7. Serve straight from the pan.
Uses the whole can of chickpeas and the whole can of tomatoes — no leftover tins.
Wednesday

Italian beef ragù with fresh pasta

25–30 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

250 g extra-lean beef mince; 1 onion, finely chopped; 1 carrot, in small cubes; 1 can chopped tomatoes (400 g); 1 tbsp tomato purée; 1 tsp dried oregano; 1 bay leaf; splash of olive oil, salt, pepper; 250 g fresh pasta (e.g. tagliatelle); 30 g grated cheese, to serve.

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion and carrot for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the mince and cook until browned and crumbly, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée, oregano and bay leaf.
  4. Simmer gently for 15 minutes with the lid ajar; season with salt and pepper.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the fresh pasta in plenty of boiling water for 2–3 minutes and drain.
  6. Toss the pasta through the ragù, or spoon the ragù over the pasta.
  7. Sprinkle with grated cheese and serve immediately.
The mince comes from a larger pack (approx. 800 g) — freeze the remaining ~500 g in 2-person portions for another week.
Thursday

Mild Thai-style chicken curry with cauliflower rice

25 min · serves 2 · wok / sauté pan

Ingredients

400 g chicken thigh fillet, cut into pieces; 1 small cauliflower, finely chopped or riced; 200 ml coconut milk; 1 tbsp mild curry powder; 1 tsp turmeric; 1 cm fresh ginger, grated; 1 courgette, in half-slices; 1 tbsp soy sauce; juice of ½ lime; fresh coriander; olive oil, salt, pepper.

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a wok or sauté pan and brown the chicken pieces on all sides, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the curry powder, turmeric and ginger and fry for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the coconut milk, add the courgette and simmer gently for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Stir in the soy sauce and lime juice; season with salt and pepper.
  5. Meanwhile, heat a splash of oil in another pan and cook the cauliflower rice for 5 minutes until just tender.
  6. Divide the cauliflower rice between two plates and spoon the curry over the top.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander.
Note: ready-made curry powder blends sometimes contain garlic powder — check the label or use loose spices (curry, turmeric, ginger).
Friday VEG

Mediterranean stuffed peppers with plant-based mince

30 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

2 large bell peppers, halved and deseeded; 250 g plant-based mince; 1 onion, chopped; 2 tbsp tomato purée; 1 tsp dried basil; salt, pepper, olive oil; 50 g grated cheese.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the plant-based mince and cook, breaking it up, for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the tomato purée and basil; season with salt and pepper.
  5. Fill the pepper halves with the mince mixture.
  6. Place the stuffed peppers in an oven dish and sprinkle with the grated cheese.
  7. Bake for 18–20 minutes until the peppers are tender and the cheese has melted.
The peppers come from the same 1+1-free pack used on Monday and Tuesday, so nothing goes to waste.
