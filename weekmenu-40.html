Recipes

Monday

Asian-Style Chicken Traybake with Peppers, Tenderstem & Ginger

25 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

350 g chicken thigh fillet, 2 red bell peppers (sliced), 200 g cocktail tomatoes (halved), 200 g tenderstem broccoli, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 2 cm fresh ginger (grated), 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp sesame seeds, black pepper.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, honey, grated ginger and sesame oil into a marinade.
  3. Cut the chicken into pieces and toss with the pepper strips and marinade on a baking tray.
  4. Roast for 15 minutes.
  5. Add the cocktail tomatoes and tenderstem broccoli, toss and roast for another 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and season with pepper.
Tip: freeze the rest of the chicken thigh fillet (from the 900 g Aldi pack) in portions for next week — none of the deal goes to waste.
Tuesday VEG

Stuffed Peppers with Chickpeas, Spinach & Feta

28 min · serves 2 · oven

Ingredients

2 large red bell peppers, 1 can chickpeas (400 g, rinsed and drained), 100 g feta (crumbled), 100 g fresh spinach, 2 eggs, 1/2 onion (finely chopped), 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp dried oregano, juice of 1/2 lemon, olive oil.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C, halve the peppers and remove the seeds and membranes.
  2. Sauté the onion until translucent in olive oil and add the spinach until wilted.
  3. Stir in the chickpeas, cumin, oregano and lemon juice, and warm through for 2 minutes.
  4. Spoon the filling into the pepper halves and crumble the feta on top.
  5. Bake the stuffed peppers for 20 minutes until tender.
  6. Meanwhile fry or poach the eggs and place one on each portion for extra protein.
Note: this recipe is garlic-free. If you swap in a ready-made spice mix instead of loose spices, always check the label for hidden garlic.
Wednesday

Italian-Style Pasta with Beef Ragù & Courgette

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

250 g pasta (e.g. De Cecco), 300 g ground beef (e.g. 2 lean beef burger patties, crumbled), 1 courgette (diced), 1 can chopped tomatoes (390 g), 1/2 onion (finely chopped), fresh basil, 1 tsp dried oregano, olive oil, grated Parmesan to serve.

Method

  1. Cook the pasta al dente according to the package instructions.
  2. Crumble the ground beef and fry until loose and browned in a splash of olive oil.
  3. Add the onion and courgette and fry for 3 to 4 minutes.
  4. Pour in the chopped tomatoes, season with oregano and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  5. Toss the drained pasta through the sauce and stir in the fresh basil.
  6. Serve with grated Parmesan.
This is the week's carb day — the other days are deliberately kept low-carb.
Thursday

French-Style Warm Steak Salad with Mustard Dressing

25 min · serves 2 · oven/air fryer + hob

Ingredients

300 g entrecôte steak, 150 g mixed salad greens, 100 g cocktail tomatoes (halved), 1/2 red onion (thinly sliced), 300 g carrots (peeled), 1 tsp mustard, 1 tbsp white wine vinegar, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp honey, salt and pepper.

Method

  1. Preheat the oven or air fryer to 200°C and roast the carrots for 15 to 18 minutes until tender and lightly caramelised.
  2. Slice the entrecôte into thin strips and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a pan over high heat and sear the strips for 2 to 3 minutes on all sides, then let rest briefly.
  4. Whisk the mustard, vinegar, olive oil and honey into a dressing.
  5. Divide the salad greens, cocktail tomatoes and red onion between the plates.
  6. Top with the warm steak strips, drizzle with the dressing and serve with the roasted carrots.
Friday VEG

Mild Coconut Lentil Dahl with Spinach & Boiled Egg

25 min · serves 2 · hob

Ingredients

1 can lentils (400 g, rinsed and drained), 1/2 onion (finely chopped), 2 cm fresh ginger (grated), 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, 200 ml coconut milk, 100 g carrot (in half-moons), 100 g fresh spinach, 2 eggs, fresh coriander or parsley and lemon juice to serve.

Method

  1. Boil the eggs for 7 to 8 minutes, rinse under cold water and peel.
  2. Sauté the onion until translucent, then add the ginger, turmeric, cumin and coriander and fry for 1 minute.
  3. Add the carrot and fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in the lentils and coconut milk and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  5. Stir through the spinach until wilted.
  6. Halve the eggs, place on top of the dahl and finish with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
